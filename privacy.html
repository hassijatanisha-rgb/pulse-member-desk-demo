Privacy

This public demonstration does not send enquiry details to a server. Fictional entries are stored only in this browser tab using session storage and disappear when the tab's session ends.

The demo has no analytics, advertising pixels, account system, payment system, or inbox connector. Do not enter real names, phone numbers, health information, or confidential business information.

An installed client-owned version is a separate deployment. Its client is responsible for its privacy notice, access controls, retention period, data requests, and legal obligations before accepting real enquiries.
